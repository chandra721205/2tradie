## 📱 How to use? (Very Simple!)

### Step 1: Open Services Page
```
1. Open TRADIE app
2. Go to Producer Dashboard
3. Click on "🛠️ Services" button at top
```

### Step 2: Choose What You Need
```
You will see 6 big buttons:
- 🚜 Earth Moving
- 👷 Labor
- 💧 Borewell
- ✈️ Drone
- 📦 Equipment
- 🏠 Housing

Click on what you need
```

### Step 3: Find Service Provider
```
You will see list of people/companies who provide that service

Each one shows:
- Name
- ⭐ Rating (how good they are)
- 📍 Distance from you
- ₹ Price
- 📞 Phone number
```

### Step 4: Request Service
```
1. Click "Request" button on the one you like
2. Fill simple form:
   - What work you need
   - Where (your farm location)
   - When (which date)
   - Budget (how much you can pay)
3. Click "Send"
```

### Step 5: They Will Call You
```
The service provider will call you on your phone
Talk to them and fix the deal
```

## 💡 Smart Tips for Farmers

### Before Hiring:

1. **Check Rating** ⭐
   - Above 4.0 = Good
   - Above 4.5 = Very Good
   - Above 4.8 = Excellent

2. **Check Distance** 📍
   - Closer = Better (less travel charge)
   - Within 20 km = Good
   - More than 50 km = Ask about travel cost

3. **Check Reviews** 📝
   - See what other farmers say
   - Recent reviews are more important

4. **Compare Prices** 💰
   - Check 2-3 providers
   - Don't always choose cheapest
   - Balance price and quality

5. **Call First** 📞
   - Talk on phone before booking
   - Confirm they understand your work
   - Discuss final price

### During Peak Season:

- **Book Early** - 2-3 weeks before you need
- **Check Alerts** - Red/yellow warnings at top
- **Be Flexible** - Have backup options

### After Service:

- **Leave Review** - Help other farmers
- **Give Rating** - Be honest
- **Save Contact** - For future use

## 📞 Common Use Cases

### Case 1: Need JCB to Dig Pond

```
1. Click "🚜 Earth Moving Equipment"
2. Search "JCB"
3. Filter: "Your District" + "Available Now"
4. Choose one with:
   - ⭐ 4+ rating
   - 📍 Within 20 km
   - 💰 Good price
5. Click "Request"
6. Fill form:
   - "Need to dig 20×20 pond"
   - "My Farm, Village Name"
   - "Next Monday"
   - "Budget ₹10,000"
```

### Case 2: Need Workers for Harvest

```
1. Click "👷 Labor Services"
2. Choose "Unskilled Labor"
3. Filter: "Your District"
4. Look for "Labor Association" (they have many workers)
5. Check: 
   - ⭐ Rating
   - 👥 Number of workers available
   - 💰 Per person cost
6. Click "Request"
7. Fill form:
   - "Need 20 workers for wheat harvest"
   - "5 acres, Village Name"
   - "Starting next week Monday, 3 days work"
   - "Budget ₹400 per person per day"
8. Send request
9. They will arrange workers
```

### Case 3: Borewell Not Working

```
1. Click "💧 Borewell & Irrigation"
2. Choose "Borewell Maintenance"
3. Filter: "Available Now" (urgent!)
4. Choose verified provider with:
   - ✅ Certified badge
   - ⭐ High rating
   - 📍 Nearby
5. Call them first (urgent matter)
6. Then send request with details
```

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Is this free to use?
**A:** Yes! Searching and contacting providers is FREE. You only pay the provider for their actual service.

### Q2: How do I pay?
**A:** Pay directly to the service provider (cash/UPI/bank). TRADIE doesn't take payment.

### Q3: What if provider doesn't come?
**A:** Call our support: 1800-XXX-XXXX. We will help you.

### Q4: Can I add my local trusted provider?
**A:** Yes! Click "Add Provider" button. Fill their details. We will verify and add them.

### Q5: Why some providers show "Busy"?
**A:** They are currently working somewhere. You can still send request - they might accept for later dates.

### Q6: What is "Grok Trust Score"?
**A:** Computer gives safety score (0-100). Higher = More trustworthy. Above 80 = Very good.

### Q7: How to know if price is fair?
**A:** Compare 2-3 providers. Check reviews. Ask other farmers in your village.

### Q8: Can I cancel request?
**A:** Yes, call the provider and inform them. Be polite.

## 📊 Understanding Pricing

### Earth Moving (JCB/Tractors):
- **Hourly:** ₹800 - ₹1,500 per hour
- **Daily:** ₹5,000 - ₹10,000 per day
- **Includes:** Fuel, operator
- **You pay:** Travel charges extra (usually)

### Labor:
- **Unskilled:** ₹300 - ₹500 per person per day
- **Skilled:** ₹600 - ₹1,000 per person per day
- **Includes:** Their labor only
- **You provide:** Food, water (usually)

### Borewell:
- **Drilling:** ₹100 - ₹200 per foot
- **Maintenance:** ₹500 - ₹2,000 fixed
- **Pump Install:** ₹3,000 - ₹8,000
- **Includes:** Work only
- **You buy:** Pump, pipes separately

### Drone:
- **Per acre:** ₹2,000 - ₹5,000
- **Includes:** Drone, operator, report
- **You get:** Photos, health report

### Equipment Rental:
- **Harvester:** ₹5,000 - ₹12,000 per day
- **Sprayer:** ₹500 - ₹1,500 per day
- **Seeder:** ₹1,000 - ₹3,000 per day
- **Includes:** Machine, operator (usually)
