## 🎯 Quick Decision Guide

### Should I Store or Sell?

**Store if**:
- AI predicts price increase
- Seasonal demand coming
- You need better price
- Storage cost < expected profit

**Sell Directly if**:
- Need immediate cash
- Current prices are good
- Storage costs too high
- Commodity is perishable

**Use Agent if**:
- Want expert negotiation
- Don't know market well
- Need better connections
- Commission < potential gain

**Use Marketplace if**:
- Want wide buyer reach
- Comfortable with digital
- Want transparent pricing
- Have time to wait
