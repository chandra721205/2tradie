### F. Common User Questions

**Q: Can I change my assigned agent later?**  
A: Yes! Click "Change Agent" in your agent dashboard. Your new agent will receive all context from the previous agent.

**Q: What if I want both storage AND direct sale?**  
A: Choose "Store First, Sell Later" in the Sell tab. You can access all selling routes after storing.

**Q: How accurate are the AI price predictions?**  
A: Our AI achieves 85% accuracy within ±3% margin for 7-day forecasts, and 75% accuracy for 30-day forecasts.

**Q: Do I have to pay the agent separately?**  
A: No! Agent services are free for producers. Agents earn commission from facilities/buyers.

**Q: Can I use the dashboard offline?**  
A: Basic functionality works offline. AI insights and real-time data require internet connection.

**Q: How do I know if a facility is verified?**  
A: Look for the ✅ verified badge and compliance certifications (FSSAI, WDRA, ISO).
