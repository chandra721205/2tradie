# 🚀 TRADIE Role System - Quick Guide

**Last Updated:** October 19, 2025

---

## 🎯 Which Role Should I Choose?

### **Choose Producer (FREE)** if you:
- Are just starting out
- Want to test the platform
- Sell occasionally
- Have small quantities
- Don't need analytics

### **Choose Producer Plus (₹999/year)** if you:
- Sell regularly
- Need bulk management
- Want price predictions
- Require analytics
- Want priority visibility
- Need reduced commissions

### **Choose Commission Agent (₹2,499/year)** if you:
- Operate as a trader
- Manage multiple producers
- Need team collaboration
- Buy & sell commodities
- Track commissions
- Run trading agency

## ⚡ Quick Actions

### **Upgrade from Producer to Producer Plus:**
1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Upgrade"
3. Choose "Producer Plus"
4. Pay ₹999
5. Done! Features unlocked instantly

### **Become Commission Agent:**
1. Complete Producer Plus first
2. Click "Marketing & Procurement"
3. Pay ₹2,499
4. Add team members (optional)
5. Start trading!

### **Add Team Member:**
1. Go to Team Management
2. Click "Add Member"
3. Enter name & mobile
4. Member receives OTP
5. Verify & add
6. Done!

## 🎓 FAQs

**Q: Can I change roles later?**  
A: Yes! Switch anytime. Your data stays intact.

**Q: Do team members need to pay?**  
A: No. Only the main account holder pays.

**Q: What happens if I don't renew?**  
A: You'll downgrade to Producer (free) tier.

**Q: Can I add more than 5 team members?**  
A: Contact support for enterprise pricing.

**Q: Is there a monthly payment option?**  
A: Yes, but annual saves 30%.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**  
A: Yes, 30-day money-back guarantee.
